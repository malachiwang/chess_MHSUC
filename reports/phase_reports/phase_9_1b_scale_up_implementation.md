## 1. ELI5 / TLDR

The pilot's key finding (8.2B) — clock features still carry predictive
signal after controlling for engine eval and ratings — rested on a CI that
barely excluded zero. This ticket scaled the dataset 10× (250k raw games,
65,368 primary positions) and reran the identical logistic ablation.

**Result: CONFIRMED.** E3 → E4 delta = **−0.0138 — the exact pilot point
estimate — with the CI narrowing from [−0.0266, −0.0002] to
[−0.0186, −0.0091]**, now comfortably excluding zero. Bonus findings: the
two pilot-unresolved comparisons (E1→E3 rating-after-eval, E4→E5
context-after-clock) now also exclude zero, and a new per-checkpoint
diagnostic shows the clock signal concentrates in late checkpoints
(move_30/40 exclude zero; move_10/20 do not). All predictive associations,
no causal claims.

## 13. Scientific Caveats

- The 250k sample is a **superset** of the pilot — scale-robustness, not
  independent cross-month external validity. External validity beyond
  2024-09 is not established.
- Player-level leakage remains unresolved (no usernames stored; same player
  may span splits) and grows with scale.
- Stockfish depth=12 eval is objective position context, not ground truth,
  and not a causal variable. Residual rating/clock associations after
  conditioning on eval are predictive associations, not causal effects.
- Degradation curves are not established; per-checkpoint deltas are
  diagnostics.
- DRAW is not solved: 603/9,823 val rows, and unweighted LR still predicts
  zero DRAW argmax rows.
- Test split untouched for selection (WORKFLOW.md §13).
- Phase 10 not started.
